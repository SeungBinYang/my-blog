{%- comment -%} Hero graphic: a skateboard deck seen from underneath, 1990s graphic. Inline SVG rather than an image file on purpose -- it paints from the theme tokens (var(--accent), var(--fg) ...), so it flips with light/dark on its own and there is no asset to keep in sync with the palette. Construction notes, since the geometry is deliberate rather than eyeballed: - The silhouette is a popsicle deck, not a rounded rectangle: the rails pull in slightly at the wheelbase, and nose and tail are true semicircles (the arc radius is exactly half the rail-to-rail height, so the caps meet the rails without a kink). - The art is clipped to a path inset 9px from the outline, leaving the bare rim a real deck has, and a pinline is stroked on that same inset path so the art ends on a crisp edge instead of just stopping. - Two fields meeting on one diagonal, not three separate blocks. The first pass had dead gaps between the blocks, which read as unfinished rather than graphic. - Trucks poke out from under the deck to meet the wheels; the wheels are seen from above, so they run longer along the board than across it (a 54mm wheel is about 32mm wide). - The star is a true five-point star: ten points alternating between an outer radius of 36 and an inner radius of 13.75 (the 0.382 ratio that makes the arms meet at a straight edge), stepping 36 degrees each time. Purely decorative, so it is hidden from assistive tech. {%- endcomment -%}
